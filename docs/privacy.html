Privacy policy

Updated October 5, 2026.

What stays on your Mac

InputPin reads the identifiers and display names of enabled keyboard input sources and the current input source. It checks whether secure input is active, so it can pause restoration. Your selected input source and pinning preference are stored locally.

No collection or tracking

The app does not collect keystrokes, typed text, passwords, account information, analytics, advertising identifiers, or crash reports for its developers. It has no account system, ads, or third-party SDKs. It sends no app data to a server. A temporary mouse-only event monitor dismisses an open panel; no global keyboard events are monitored.

Your controls

Pause pinning at any time. Launch at login is optional; turn it off before uninstalling. Local preferences remain until you delete the app's preferences or sandbox container. There is no developer-held personal data to delete.

Links and distribution

Opening a GitHub, support, or privacy link uses your browser. Those websites have their own privacy policies. Apple and GitHub may process information associated with their distribution services independently of InputPin.

Contact

For questions, open a GitHub issue. Do not include passwords or sensitive typed text.

隐私政策

InputPin 仅在本机读取已启用输入源的名称、标识符、当前输入源和安全输入状态。所选输入源与固定设置保存在本机，不向服务器发送应用数据。

不采集按键、输入内容、密码、账户信息或分析数据，不包含广告、账户系统或第三方 SDK。弹窗打开时仅临时监听鼠标点击以关闭弹窗，不全局监听键盘事件。

你可随时暂停固定。开机启动须主动开启，卸载前可先关闭。本地偏好在删除应用偏好或沙盒容器后清除；开发者没有保存需要删除的个人数据。外部链接和下载渠道遵循其各自的隐私政策。问题可通过 GitHub issue 联系，请勿提交密码或敏感输入内容。
