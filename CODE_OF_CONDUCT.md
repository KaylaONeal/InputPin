# Code of conduct

## Our commitment

We want a welcoming, respectful community for contributors of every background, identity and experience level. Participate with empathy, assume good intent, and keep discussions focused on the work.

## Expected behavior

- Use considerate language and accept constructive feedback.
- Respect differing viewpoints and lived experiences.
- Take responsibility for mistakes and help resolve disagreements.
- Respect privacy, consent and the community's time.

## Unacceptable behavior

Harassment, discrimination, threats, personal attacks, sexualized language, unwelcome attention, doxxing, and publishing private information without consent are not acceptable. The policy applies to repository issues, pull requests, discussions and other spaces representing this project.

## Reporting and enforcement

For private concerns, use the maintainers' contact options on their GitHub profiles. If no private channel is available, report abusive content using GitHub's built-in reporting tools or [GitHub Support](https://support.github.com/). Do not publish personal details in an issue.

Maintainers may remove harmful content, request a correction, warn participants, temporarily restrict participation, or permanently exclude repeat or severe offenders. Decisions should be proportionate, explain the violated standard, and protect reporters from retaliation. Participants can request reconsideration through the same private reporting route. A maintainer involved in a complaint should not adjudicate it.

This policy draws on the values of the [Contributor Covenant](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/). It is a project-specific policy, not a claim of verbatim adoption.
